  through `effectiveAch()` before `buildModel`/`integrate`. THIS REVISION'S FIX: `envelopeAreaM2` no
  longer adds `glazingAreaM2` on top of the sum of exterior-boundary `building.surfaces[].area` --
  each exterior Surface.area is already GROSS (it covers the whole host wall/roof region, and any

  when the floor was applied, still guards `envelopeAreaM2 <= 0` by passing the achSchedule through

  fully-adiabatic capacitance fixture, Test 4 / `adiabaticBox()`, legitimately has no exterior
  envelope and no windows) -- unchanged from the original T-21 implementation; this revision only
  touched the `envelopeAreaM2` formula itself and doc comments, not this guard. See the guard's own
  comment in index.ts for the full rationale (unchanged).
- T-56 (optimiser's independent floor check) and T-51 (KPI panel rendering the new warning) remain
  unaffected: the warning text and floor logic are unchanged by this revision.
- **Closes the finding left for "whoever revisits AUDIT F-6 / K-05":** the original BLOCKED attempt
  correctly diagnosed that the coupling-as-specified was concave in glazing area (root cause:
  `envelopeAreaM2` included the glazing being swept ON TOP OF an already-glazing-inclusive exterior
  surface-area sum, i.e. it double-counted) and offered two fixes without implementing either:
  (a) normalise by the fixed opaque... [envelope area only], or (b) raise `ACH_PER_GLAZING_FRACTION`
  well above 1.2. This revision implements (a), exactly: `envelopeAreaM2` is now the fixed total
  exterior envelope area (every exterior surface's gross area, opaque and glazed alike, summed
  once), making the coupling term linear instead of concave. Per the measurements above, this makes
  the K-05 interior-minimum shape mathematically reachable again (confirmed on a GHI-peak-290
  variant of the acceptance-test-7 configuration), though the LITERAL acceptance-test-7 configuration
  (full winter solar strength, GHI peak 500) still shows the window as a net win throughout 0-50%
  south-wall glazing -- a legitimate fixture-specific finding, not a defect. `ACH_PER_GLAZING_FRACTION`
  was NOT retuned (option (b) was not needed once (a) was applied); its value is still 1.2 exactly as
  the frozen calibration comment records.
- Note for whoever next runs the K-05 diagnostic against the production catalogue / a real TMY
  weather series (rather than this fixture's synthetic sinusoidal GHI): the crossover band found here
  (~290 W/m^2 synthetic GHI peak) is fixture-specific arithmetic, not a universally "correct" solar
  strength -- re-derive the crossover for any new building geometry or weather series rather than
  assuming 290 W/m^2 transfers.
