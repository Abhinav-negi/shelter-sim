Status: IN-PROGRESS      Depends on: A1

## Goal
(1) The A1 "Run on ANSYS" dialog renders top-left: Tailwind v4 preflight's `margin:0` kills native `<dialog>`
centering. Center it. (2) Cancelling an ANSYS run needs a second confirm popup, in Studio and Dashboard. (3) A logged-in
visitor at `/` goes straight to `/app`. (4) Log out in the app header. Client only; JWT cookie auth is unchanged.
