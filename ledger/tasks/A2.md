**Root cause / fix (Goal 1).** Tailwind v4 preflight sets `margin:0` on every element, which cancels the
native `<dialog>` UA-stylesheet `margin:auto` centering — that's why A1's inline dialog rendered top-left.
`ConfirmDialog` (condition 1) restores it with `m-auto` in its class list, plus the rest of A1's styling
(`w-[min(24rem,calc(100vw-2rem))] rounded-sm border border-hairline bg-paper p-5 text-ink backdrop:bg-ink/40`)
moved out of Studio.tsx. It wraps native `<dialog>` behind `{open, message, confirmLabel, cancelLabel,
onConfirm, onClose, busy?}`: a `useEffect` calls `showModal()`/`close()` to track `open`, and the dialog's
native `onClose` (fired on Esc) invokes the `onClose` prop.

**Regression caught and fixed during verification.** AppShell's new "Log out" button used the shared `Button`
primitive's default `px-4 py-2`; even with a `className="px-0 py-0 text-sm"` override, computed padding stayed
8px/8px — Tailwind utilities of equal specificity are ordered by the compiled stylesheet, not by class-list
position, so plain `py-0` lost to Button's own `py-2`. That grew the shared header from ~54px to ~60px, which
broke Studio.tsx's hardcoded `h-[calc(100dvh-56px)]` (S1's fixed-height invariant) and made the Studio page
scroll by 4px (measured: `document.documentElement.scrollHeight` 904 vs `innerHeight` 900, confirmed via a
disposable inspection script; baseline before my change was 900/900). Fixed by using Tailwind's `!` important
modifier (`!px-0 !py-0`) on the Log out button in `AppShell.tsx`, which reliably wins the cascade — re-measured
header content height back to 18.56px (matching the "Studio" link) and `scrollHeight` back to 900.

**Condition 6 / flow.mjs.** The only line that needed to change: after condition 5 moved Log out to AppShell
and pointed it at `navigate('/')` instead of the old Dashboard `navigate('/login')`, flow.mjs's step 9 assertion
(`page.waitForURL(...)`) was updated from `/login` to `/` to match. flow.mjs never visits bare `/` while logged
in elsewhere in the flow, so no other change was needed there.
